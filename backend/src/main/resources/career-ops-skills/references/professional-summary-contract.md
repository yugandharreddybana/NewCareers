# Professional Summary — mandatory contract

Every tailored CV **must** use this structure for the Professional Summary section.
This applies to the HTML resume (`{{SUMMARY}}`), the JSON `summary` field, and the
`sections[]` entry named "Professional Summary".

## Required structure (all three elements, in this order)

Write **exactly three sentences**. Each sentence maps to one element below.
Do not merge elements. Do not skip any element. Do not reorder.

### 1. Who you are

A short introduction: the candidate's **profession**, **background**, or **area of expertise**
(e.g. years of experience + core identity relevant to the target role).

### 2. Key skills / expertise

The **main skills** the candidate has gained in past or current roles that are **directly relevant**
to the role they are applying for. Mirror language from the job description where natural.

### 3. Value you bring

How the candidate can **add value** to **this specific company or role** — outcomes, impact, or
contribution aligned to what the employer is hiring for.

## Example shape (adapt content to the candidate and JD)

> [Who you are — one sentence.]
> [Key skills/expertise — one sentence, JD-aligned.]
> [Value you bring — one sentence, employer-specific.]

## Quality rules

- Third person or implied first person is fine; avoid "I am writing to express…"
- Weave JD keywords into sentences 2 and 3 naturally — no keyword stuffing
- Irish English spelling
- No banned AI clichés (leveraged, spearheaded, passionate about, team player, etc.)
